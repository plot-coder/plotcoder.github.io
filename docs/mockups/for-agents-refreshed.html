3 · Is this the best we can do?

One sheet or two. "Your agent" (the three steps) and this sheet could be one. Kept apart: most people need the three steps and nothing here, and folding this into that would put six kinds of setup in front of someone who wanted one. This sheet's first card sends them there.
The words stay the agent's. The on-ramp at llms.txt is what an agent reads, and every round of testing has tuned it. The refresh changes what a person is shown, not what an agent is told: the full text is one tap away in each tab, word for word.
What it costs. Each door needs one short sentence for a person, beside the paragraph it has for an agent. That is six new sentences to keep true when the doors change. A test can hold each to its door.
Not drawn. The sign-in for the door (R72). When it exists, the Claude app tab is an address and two buttons, and the header goes.
The name. "For agents" becomes "Agents": the sheet is for the person as much as the agent.

What a yes means. "Build it as drawn" builds the two cards, the link to hand over, the four tabs with a short line and a Copy each, the three folds, and the full on-ramp text behind each "the whole of it". "One sheet" folds Your agent's three steps into this sheet as its first tab, and the button under the wordmark opens it. Anything else, say what, and it is drawn again first.
