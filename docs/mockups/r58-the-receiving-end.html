Recommended: B, the corner folded in with the edge line, and one record on the fold's card. The corner is already what a plant looks like and already the one gesture for it, so the payoff is the same mark turned the other way and the same tap; A is B without the corner, and loses the glance from Fit zoom; C draws a thing that cannot be dragged. The record stays on the fold because a plant is the fold's claim — later already lives there — and two records for one claim would be the thing the reading was built to avoid. The receiving board composes its marks from the project, the way it composes the cast on other boards (R51), so the kernel gains one field, payoffNoteId, and no second model. The fold's check changes meaning: a board's name is a promise, a scene is the payoff, and the wall asks until it has the scene.

Is this the best we could do? Two things B cannot say. A card that pays off two folds from other boards gets one corner and an edge line that has to read "Pays off · 2 folds"; the picker can hold both, the edge cannot name both. And the page still says nothing: a script has no place for a plant, and Fountain's note is the only carrier, so the payoff lives on the wall and in the reading, not on the page. If either matters, say so; otherwise B is the card, and the tool comes first.
