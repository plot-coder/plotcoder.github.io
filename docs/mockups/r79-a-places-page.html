R79: a place's page

Drawn 2026-09-27 from pass 4a (blind-runs/pass-4a-report.md, entries 7, 18, 45, and its question 9). A person has a page — looks, voice, wants, needs, notes, what is open, a picture — and every card they are on. A place has a phrase on a card and nothing else: set_location takes the words, the app relates no two, and every fact a shoot needs about the harbour office — its size, its window, what is on its walls, the era of its fittings — has nowhere to live but the first line of some scene's action. What follows is the page a place would have, drawn in the cast lens's own paper beside a person's; where it opens from; the tools; and the question. The reading asks nothing of a place: a page fills when the writer writes on it, and an empty one asks its questions the way a person's does.

What it costs, and one rule. A record on the project, three tools, a page in the lens, the brief's line, one section of the writer's guide. The rule: the phrase is the key. "INT. THE HARBOUR OFFICE" and "the harbour office" are two places until the writer says otherwise, as two spellings of a name are two people until renamed — and the same fix applies: rename one to the other and the cards follow, the pages merge, the first line written wins. A place's page is not a slugline's: the page is the writer's words and the heading prints the phrase as before.

Recommended: as drawn — a page that fills when written on, keyed by the phrase, in the lens the places already sit in, with the person's gestures and the person's tools renamed. The alternatives: a note on the first card at that place (a drawer nobody reads, and the brief prints the first card's note for every scene there); the premise for the era alone (true of the film, not of a place, and the office's window is not the premise's); a separate "locations" sheet with its own list (a second place where places live — the combine log's rule says no).

Is this the best we could do? Two things this does not do. It does not relate an interior to its exterior ("INT. THE HARBOUR OFFICE" and "EXT. THE HARBOUR OFFICE" are two pages; a writer who wants one can name the place without the prefix and let the heading's INT./EXT. come from the card, which is a rule the app does not have yet). And it does not give a place a picture the video tool can hold across two hundred segments — that is 4b's question, and the page is where such a picture would live. Say whether the page waits for the day (R78) or goes first; it is the larger of the two and the one the reader asked for most.
