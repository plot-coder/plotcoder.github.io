R80: a scene's shots

Drawn 2026-09-27 on Robert's need: take the script, and make every shot that goes into a scene. Decided with him the same day: a shot is a line of the script, its files hang off the shot's id, the agent's breakdown stands and the record says whose, and the stills are made outside the app from a prompt the app hands over. The agent's half is built; this page is the person's half, for his word before it is built.

1 · The moment: a scene with shots on it, as it ships today

As it ships. The agent's tools write the shot lines into the scene's text. In the app a person sees them as they are typed: a note in double brackets, in the scene editor and in Pages. Nothing prints, nothing is measured, and nothing on the screen says there is a still.

Every word is the wall's: the shot's line, the place's page, the person's looks, the light, the look. When the wall holds no looks for someone in the shot, the sheet says so under the shot and leaves them out; it invents nothing.

The pictures coming back

One way in, to start: the sheet. Drop a file on a shot, or drop several on the sheet and the ones named for a shot find it. The app keeps them on the project as it keeps a person's pictures. Nothing to connect, no second place to look.

Held until the size asks for it: pictures kept somewhere else and linked. A project's files sit in one bucket with a 200 MB limit a file; a still is two or three, so a film of a thousand stills is two or three gigabytes, which is when to decide where they go.

4 · What is not drawn, and why

No strip of frames on the wall or the card. A card is a scene; eight frames on forty cards is a wall nobody can read. The card gets nothing new. If the takes' mark on a card (the small film mark it has today) should also say "has shots", that is one glyph, and yours to ask for.
No editing of a still. The edit is made in the tool that made it; the edited file comes back as another still on the same shot, and the newest is the first frame unless you choose.
No video from the sheet yet. The video tool is still question 26. When there is one, a shot with a still gets one more button, and its takes show under its frame as a scene's do in Takes.

What a yes means. "2 A and the sheet" builds the mark in Pages and the Shots sheet as drawn, with the drop by file name, and writes the writer's guide for both. "2 B and the sheet" builds the sheet alone. Anything else, say what, and it is drawn again first.
