3 · Is this the best we can do?

How the app knows nobody chose. A board holds a number, 120 by default, and nothing says whether a person put it there. So the board gains one fact, whether its length was chosen. A board made before this that still holds exactly 120, with no kind and no open words, reads as not chosen: a writer who typed 120 by hand will be asked once more. One press of "A feature" answers it.
The agent's side still says 120. The readings an agent gets say "of 120 pages" on a board with no length chosen. The same honesty belongs there; it is a change to the server's words and goes with the next release. Not in this build.
The bar's number stays. The readout in the bar of icons keeps its number to type over; on a board with no length chosen it shows the pages alone. Two ways to the same fact, both in view when they are in view.
Not drawn: a length for the whole series. Six episodes of an hour is six boards of sixty pages. The project's total is already read out by an agent's read_project; nothing in the app sums it for a person yet.

What a yes means. The count beside the story map's tab becomes the control, with the five choices; a board with no length chosen says "Set a length" and shows no target; the writer's guide says where.
