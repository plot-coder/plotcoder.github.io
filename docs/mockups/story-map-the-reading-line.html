The cost. Every selection moves the wall: the cards in the right-hand picture stand 30 pixels higher than in the left. And pointing at a block on the strip makes the map taller, which moves the block out from under the pointer; the map then closes, the block comes back, and it opens again.

What a yes means. "A" builds the floating line: the map is 52 pixels with no beats and no groups, the count rides in the tab, the hint goes, and nothing moves when a card is read. "B" builds the map that grows on a selection, and only on a selection, not on a pointer over the strip, so it cannot flicker; the wall still rises and falls with it. "Leave it" keeps the 82 that ships.
