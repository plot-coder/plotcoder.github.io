4 · Is this the best we can do?

An "Edit" mode for the whole list, as a phone's lists have, was the other way: one button turns every row into a field with a delete mark. Decided against: it makes deleting one tap from six projects at once, and it hides the row's facts just when they are needed to choose the right twin.
Telling twins apart. Renaming would not have helped Robert here: he could not tell which of three to rename. So each row says what is in the project and who changed it last and when. That line is the record the wall already keeps (R76).
What it costs. The counts and the last change need each project's boards read when the sheet opens, where today it reads one row a project. On an account of six that is nothing; on sixty it wants the counts kept on the project's row. Drawn as if they are there.
Not drawn. Deleting several at once; a bin that holds a deleted project for a while. The second is the safer design and the larger build; say if you want it and Delete becomes "Move to the bin".
Agents. An agent already has rename_project and delete_project, so this brings the person level with the agent, not the other way.

What a yes means. "Build it as drawn" builds the order (projects, your agent, you, delete account last), the facts on each row, the row that opens with rename, open, download and delete, the people inside the open project's row, and the writer's guide for it. "With a bin" makes delete recoverable for thirty days. Anything else, say what, and it is drawn again first.
