4 · Is this the best we can do?

A tour was decided against when the on-ramp was first built (R43): a door, not a tour. A is not a tour: it is three buttons on a wall that has nothing else on it, and it is gone with the first card.
What to say to the agent. The sentence on the first card is the app's own workflow, "Break a treatment into a wall", which an agent already knows by name. The card copies it; the writer adds their notes.
What it does not fix. The agent's button stays a small pill under the wordmark. With the cut edge mended and the left edge lined up, I think it is right at that size: it is a status, and A gives the agent its weight on an empty wall.

What a yes means. "Build A" builds the three ways in the middle of an empty wall, the agent's card first and lit when one is here, and takes the corner's words away. "Build B" tidies the corner and leaves the middle empty. The button's cut edge is mended either way.
