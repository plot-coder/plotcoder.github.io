3 · Is this the best we can do?

What is lost. Typing a logline straight onto the wall's head, without opening anything. It becomes one press and then type. I think that is a fair price for a title that can never cover the wall or run off it.
What the three links were for. I built them two changes ago to make the fields say what they are. They did, and they still appeared and vanished under the pointer. The panel keeps their words and drops the hiding.
How it would be tested. The same seven states at the same widths, by the check that found the faults above, before it is merged, and the panel seen with a filled logline, a premise and open questions in it.

What a yes means. "Build the panel" makes the title area one line that opens the story panel as drawn, with a Story button on narrow windows, and takes out the links and the boxes under the logline.
