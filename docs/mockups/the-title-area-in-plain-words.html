3 · Is this the best we can do?

The words change, the things do not. The logline, its open, the open lines and the premise are what they were, held where they were. "Not decided yet, about the film" becomes "Open questions about the story" on the wall; an agent's tools and the guide keep their own names for it.
"Not decided yet…" stays on the cards. On a card's place, time and cast the phrase sits beside the one field it is about, and reads plainly there. It was only in the title area, with two of them a line apart, that it confused.
An example is a claim about what a logline is. The one drawn is a question; a logline can be a statement. It is offered as "for example", on an empty field only, and goes the moment you type.
What it does not do. It does not teach the method. The Help sheet and the writer's guide do that; this makes the four fields say what they are.

What a yes means. The labels as drawn, the hint under an empty logline, the three ways on shown as plain links and opened one at a time, each field with a sentence saying what it is.
