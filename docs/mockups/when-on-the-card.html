When a scene happens, on the card (R55)

The same card three ways, at the size it sits on the wall. The scene is the pier at Fenit, night, day four; the kernel and the tools already hold the when. What is undecided is where a person sees and types it.

Recommended: A. The heading is "THE PIER AT FENIT - NIGHT", and the place line is the heading's home on the card, so the when belongs on it, after a dot; a person types "the pier at Fenit · night" or leaves the dot out. It adds nothing to the card's height and no new gesture. B is the honest fallback if the one line reads as clutter with long places. C reads best from far away but needs a control the edge does not have.

Is this the best we could do? The one thing A cannot say is "when unknown" as distinct from nothing; if that matters, B's empty line ("when …" on hover) says it better. The headline stops carrying the day, which changes the guide's convention: the day goes in the when, and the headline is the scene.
